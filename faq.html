Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most searched questions by Punjab University BSCS students.

Where can I find the PU BSCS course outline for affiliated colleges?

You can find the complete, official Punjab University (PU) BSCS course outline and syllabus breakdowns on our dedicated Course Outline page. It covers all 3rd-semester subjects.

Are 3rd-semester BSCS notes and 2nd-year computer notes free to download?

Yes! We provide complete 3rd-semester BSCS notes, including lab manuals and reference books. All PDFs are 100% free to download from our Notes Repository.

Do you have OOP, COAL, and Calculus past papers?

Absolutely. Our Past Papers archive includes OOP past papers for Punjab University (CC 211), COAL past papers (DC-221), and Calculus past papers (MS 251) to help you prepare for your final exams.

Do you provide BSCS 1st semester past papers or 7th-semester notes?

Currently, our primary focus is on the 3rd semester. However, we are actively working on expanding our database to include BSCS 1st semester past papers, 7th-semester notes, and other study materials for all PU affiliated colleges very soon.

Is this an official Punjab University website?

No. This platform is an independent educational initiative created by a dedicated student to support peers in PU affiliated colleges. It is not officially endorsed by Punjab University.
